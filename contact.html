FAQ

Good to Know

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2:00pm and check-out is by 11:00am. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged on request, subject to availability.

Is there parking at the hotel?

Yes. Secure parking is free for hotel and conference guests.

Do you offer airport transfers?

We can arrange transfers from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and Wilson Airport. Please tell us your flight details at least 24 hours ahead.

Are pets allowed?

Sorry, pets are not allowed in the rooms. Service animals are always welcome.
